Refind: A memory, made searchable.

Every screenshot you take is an act of intention: a receipt, an address, a flight confirmation, a quote that stopped you mid-scroll. You saved it because it mattered. But mobile galleries turn that intention into an infinite scroll of dread.

Refind turns screenshot capture into instant recall: a 100% on-device, private search engine designed to find your screenshots the instant you type what you remember.

Key Highlights:

• Instant Private Recall Search:
Search across your entire screenshot library using natural words. A dual-leg SQLite FTS5 engine with prefix and trigram tokenization surfaces exact matches as you type.

• On-Demand Text Extraction:
Tap into any screenshot in the viewer to crop and scan text on demand. Cleanly extract snippets into a bottom sheet with 1-tap clipboard copy and sharing.

• Instrument-Grade Transparency:
We reject the idea that software should hide telemetry. A live 180° radial arc tachometer gives you full visibility over your library's indexing progress directly in the header.

• 100% On-Device & Zero Cloud:
The cloud does not deserve your private screenshots. Refind has no remote servers, no user accounts, no tracking SDKs, and zero telemetry. All text processing and indexing runs strictly on your phone's processor.

• Native High-Throughput In-Tree OCR:
Powered by a custom Kotlin coroutines JSI module (expo-mlkit-ocr) with bundled offline Google ML Kit models (Latin/English script, with Devanagari on the way) for fast, network-free indexing.

• Native Material You Experience:
Fluid 120Hz gesture physics (pinch-to-zoom, pan, dismiss), adaptive dark and light theming, and edge-to-edge layout.
